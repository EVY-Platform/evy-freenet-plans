# AGENTS instructions for freenet-plans

## Always

Respond or write to the user in plain language: follow ISO 24495 and the rules below.
Follow /unslop skill guideliens for wording and syntax

## Avoid

Historic or negated old text: "Old approach was x", "We won't do x", "We can't do x". We only ever want to mention things that we WILL be doing.
Puffery: "pivotal moment", "testament to", "evolving landscape". Cut puffery, state what happened.
Promotional language: "nestled", "vibrant", "breathtaking". Use neutral descriptions.
Vague attributions: "Experts believe", "Industry reports suggest", "Some critics argue". Name the source or delete.
Formulaic challenges: "Despite challenges... continues to thrive." Replace with specific facts.
Em dash overuse: Avoid em dashes entirely.
Colon and semi-colon overuse: Fine before a list or example. Not as mid-sentence connectors.
Boldface overuse: Don't bold every proper noun or acronym.
Inline-header lists: The tell is a bold label and colon that restates the line
Title case headings: Use sentence case.
Curly quotes: Replace with straight quotes.
Chatbot phrases: "I hope this helps!", "Let me know if...", "Of course!". Remove.
Cutoff disclaimers: "While specific details are limited..." Find sources or remove.
Sycophantic tone: "Great question! You're absolutely right!" Respond directly.
Filler phrases: "In order to" becomes "To". "Due to the fact that" becomes "Because". "It is important to note that" gets deleted.
Excessive hedging: "could potentially possibly be argued that it might" becomes "may".
Generic conclusions: "The future looks bright." State specific plans or facts.
Say what it does, not how it feels: "the database stays close at hand", "SQL you can read", etc. Ask what the sentence tells the reader to do or know, then write that. If you can't restate it as a concrete instruction, fact, or number, cut it.
Prefer the plain word: "utilize" becomes "use", "leverage" becomes "use", "in the event that" becomes "if". The fancier synonym is rarely clearer.
